PRIVACY & DATA

VOID SIGNAL is a free browser game without accounts, advertising, third-party analytics, or external score submissions.

Data saved on your device

The game stores mission progress, stars, best scores, audio and effects preferences, and limited Daily Signal records using your browser's localStorage. This information stays in your browser and is not sent to us by the game. You can remove it by clearing site data for this domain in browser settings.

Daily Signal

Daily challenges are generated from the UTC date in your browser. Three launches per day are counted locally. They are not tamper-proof and no public leaderboard exists.

Hosting

The game is served as static files. The hosting platform may process technical access logs according to its own policies. This page does not make promises about data handling by your browser or hosting provider.

Sound & accessibility

The game uses generated sound effects only. You can mute audio, disable screen shake and bright effects, or request reduced motion in your device settings.
