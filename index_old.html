What is WeirdFlex?

A home base for self-contained browser games with strong mood, clear mechanics and a little personality. Think indie microsites, but playable.

1
One click, instant play.
No installs, no launchers, no unnecessary friction.
2
Distinct worlds.
Each project gets its own look, feeling and tiny mythology.
3
Built to expand.
New titles can live here at simple links like /gullcity/, /nightshift/ and beyond.
